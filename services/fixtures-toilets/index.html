Questions About the Work

Why was this toilet or faucet model recommended over others?

A model may be suggested because of how it connects to the existing flange, drain and supply line, so ask which connections were looked at.

  • Ask which connections the model suits

    Which measurements and connections the model choice used is a question for the respondent. For a toilet that means the drain connection and flange; for a tap, the hole layout and the stop valves below. Photograph the area under the sink or behind the toilet, without forcing any valve, say whether the fixture is being replaced or only repaired, and send the existing model name if visible.

  • Compare repair, like-for-like swap and new model

    Compare the recommended model with repairing the fixture already installed, and with a like-for-like replacement. Ask what evidence, such as worn parts or a cracked bowl, favours each, and whether a different model would need changes to the supply or drain.

  • Record the model and what it replaces

    Ask for a written record of the model chosen, the fixtures it replaces and the connections reused or renewed, and ask what was found behind or beneath the fixture. Keep the model name and number with your papers for your Midland bathroom. Any separate concern, such as a slow drain or a weeping stop valve, stays listed as its own item so the choice of model stays clear.
